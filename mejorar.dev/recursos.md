## Libros

### Diseño y patrones
- ⭐ *Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software* — Gamma, Helm, Johnson, Vlissides (GoF).
- ⭐ *Dive Into Design Patterns* — Alexander Shvets (refactoring.guru), más digerible que el GoF.
- *Clean Code* / *Clean Architecture* — Robert C. Martin.
- *A Philosophy of Software Design* — John Ousterhout.
- *Refactoring* (2.ª ed.) — Martin Fowler.
- *Patterns of Enterprise Application Architecture* — Martin Fowler.

### Arquitectura y DDD
- ⭐ *Fundamentals of Software Architecture* — Mark Richards & Neal Ford.
- ⭐ *Learning Domain-Driven Design* — Vlad Khononov.
- *Domain-Driven Design Distilled* — Vaughn Vernon.
- *Software Architecture: The Hard Parts* — Ford, Richards, Sadalage, Dehghani.
- *Building Evolutionary Architectures* — Ford, Parsons, Kua, Sadalage.
- *Building Microservices* (2.ª ed.) — Sam Newman.
- *Monolith to Microservices* — Sam Newman.

### Sistemas distribuidos, resiliencia y redes
- ⭐ *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* — Martin Kleppmann.
- ⭐ *Release It!* (2.ª ed.) — Michael Nygard.
- ⭐ *High Performance Browser Networking* — Ilya Grigorik (gratis: hpbn.co).
- *Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach* — Kurose & Ross.

### Observabilidad y SRE
- ⭐ *Site Reliability Engineering* y *The Site Reliability Workbook* — Google (gratis: sre.google/books).
- *Observability Engineering* — Majors, Fong-Jones, Miranda.

### DevOps y entrega
- ⭐ *Accelerate* — Forsgren, Humble, Kim (origen de las métricas DORA).
- *Continuous Delivery* — Humble & Farley.
- *The DevOps Handbook* — Kim, Humble, Debois, Willis.

### Identidad
- *OAuth 2 in Action* — Richer & Sanso.
- *Solving Identity Management in Modern Applications* — Wilson & Hingnikar.
